# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 12, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the following Chrome extensions:
- **PureJSON — Offline JSON Viewer & Exporter**
- **PulseRefresh — Multi-Tab Auto Refresh & Monitor**
- **SleepTab — Safe Native Tab Suspender**
- **CleanPDF — Local Watermark-Free Page to PDF**

## Data Collection & Usage
- **Zero personal data collection:** These extensions do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing history, page contents, or analytics.
- **100% local execution:** All formatting, tab discarding, auto-refreshing, and PDF generation happen locally inside your browser.
- **Local storage only:** User preferences (such as theme, refresh intervals, or domain whitelists) are stored locally in `chrome.storage.local` on your device and are never sent to any external server.
- **No third-party tracking or ads:** None of these extensions contain ads, affiliate injectors, or third-party analytics SDKs.
